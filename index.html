F-15C SIT

The Flaming Cliffs F-15C gets the display it never had: a SIT page on the lower-left MPCD with your radar and the datalink picture, threat rings, your route and bullseye, missiles in flight with time to impact, plus menu pages for countermeasure programs, combat jettison, SRS radio presets and data cartridges.

A tech mod for the stock F-15C. Needs DCS World with the F-15C from Flaming Cliffs, nothing else. It sits in Saved Games and leaves ED's files as they are, apart from a marked block in the F-15C's controls file (its keybinds) that UNINSTALL takes out again. SRS is optional, for the radio page.

The pages

SL. 2 – SL. 11

The tactical picture

A heading-up picture around your jet, in the real SIT page's colours and symbols. Your own radar's contacts are solid, datalink contacts dotted: green friendlies, red hostiles, white unknowns, your flight in cyan with its number.

  • Datalink from AWACS, EWR, ships and datalink fighters, with sensor range, radar horizon and terrain taken into account.
  • The radar's scan edges and its cursor follow the VSD live; castle up gives the cursor to the SIT page.
  • Put the cursor on a contact for its data block: identity, bearing and range, altitude, speed and aspect.
  • On servers that block the datalink export, the server's EWR picture reports fill the page instead.

Route, threats and bullseye

The route from the mission or your cartridge with the active steerpoint marked, enemy SAM sites as rings once a friendly sensor has seen them, the bullseye, and the mission's drawings.

  • Range like the real page: 5 to 160 NM centred, 7 to 240 NM decentred, AUTO follows the radar's range.
  • EXP zooms 10 NM around the cursor, F5 frames your formation.
  • Your missiles in flight with a line to the target and the time to impact; mark points at the cursor.
  • Five declutter steps from FULL to MIN, on a keybind.

Stores and jettison

The A/A page shows every station as it is loaded, chaff, flares and gun rounds, the jammer's state and your selected countermeasure program. Two combat jettison programs pick the station groups; one keybind drops them.

Flaming Cliffs drops either the tanks or all stores: the page tells you which one a program will do.

Countermeasure programs

Six programs (PFM and PRG1–5) with chaff, flares, interval and cycles, edited right on the MPCD. Bind CMD - Dispense and CMD - Next program to your HOTAS and the jet runs the program for you.

Radios through SRS

UHF-1 for mission comms and UHF-2 for your flight, 20 presets each, from the mission or your cartridge. Step through them on the page or with keybinds, and SRS tunes along. Any preset's frequency can be changed in the cockpit.

Works with SRS's UDP command port (9040 by default).

Power-up

SL. 11

A display that needs power

On a cold start the MPCD stays dark until the jet has power: the electric power switch or an engine running. Then it runs a short self test and opens on the DTM page, ready for your cartridge. Hot and air starts come up on the SIT page.

  • The OFF / NIGHT / DAY knob below the display is clickable: OFF switches it off, NIGHT dims it.
  • The BRT rocker sets the brightness in ten steps.
  • All 20 bezel buttons are clickable and bindable.

DTM Planner

ONE HTML FILE · OFFLINE

Plan it, load it in the jet

The F-15C DTM Planner is a single HTML file that runs offline in any browser, with the DCS maps built in. Plan your flight, save the cartridge into Saved Games\DCS\DTC\F-15C, and in the jet go to DTM, pick it and press READ.

  • Route with altitudes, bearing and distance per leg, and the bullseye.
  • Threat rings, suspected threats, FLOT, borders and areas, drawn straight onto the map.
  • Radio presets for both UHF radios, CMDS programs, combat jettison groups.
  • Imports a mission file (.miz) to start from what the mission already has.
  • A SIT page preview shows the picture you will get in the cockpit.

Install

Everything lives in your Saved Games folder. The installer copies the add-on's export script and hook, adds one line to Export.lua and puts the add-on's controls into the F-15C's controls file. UNINSTALL takes all of it out again.

  1. Unzip into Mods\techDownload v1.0 and unzip it so the folder sits at Saved Games\DCS\Mods\tech\F-15C SIT (WW).
  2. Run INSTALL.batClose DCS first. Run it again after every DCS update or repair: DCS puts its own controls file back, your key assignments come back with the add-on's block.
  3. Bind and flyOptions > Controls > F-15C, category F-15C SIT: castle switch, SIT cursor, range, declutter, marks, CMDS, radios. The pilot manual explains every page.
